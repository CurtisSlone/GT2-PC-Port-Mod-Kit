GT2 AI Rubber Band

GT2 doesn't give a trailing AI car more power or more grip. It gives the car more time. Every car has a time scale that multiplies its physics step (a tick, one 1/30 s update), so an AI car at 1.10 lives 10% more time each tick than you do: same engine, same tires, same line, slightly fast-forwarded. A car ahead can be put in slight slow motion the same way. This page shows when that happens and how strong it gets on the stock events.

Time, not speed

The time scale

Each car keeps a time scale in its record, where 4096 means normal speed. GT2's physics step is 2184 units of time per tick, and each car's step is that times its scale. The rubber band writes the scale at the end of one tick and it takes effect on the next.

dt = (2184 × time_scale) >> 12

">> 12" means divide by 4096, dropping the remainder.

What it doesn't touch

The car's engine power, gearing, tire grip and the AI's driving line are unchanged. A boosted car accelerates, brakes and corners exactly as it would, just over more time per tick. That is why the effect is hard to see in any one corner and easy to see over a lap.

When it kicks in

Each event's data carries six rubber band settings plus a global scale. There are two sides, each with a start distance, a full-strength distance and a strength. Between the two distances the effect ramps up in a straight line; a full lap counts as past the full distance.

Catch-up

An AI car behind its reference car by more than the start distance runs faster, up to the full boost.

Slow-down

An AI car ahead of its reference car by more than the start distance runs slower, down to the full cut.

Player mode

Every AI car is banded against you.

Neighbor mode

Each AI car is banded against the car next to it in the running order: the car just behind for cars ahead of you, the car just ahead for cars behind you. The field gets pulled toward you like a chain.

Only some race types use it. Time trials and license tests don't. An AI car that is off the course gets no catch-up boost.

Two real events

Both curves come from real races, with the settings read back from their event data.

How strong it gets

The strongest slow-down each stock event allows, over all 263 GT Mode and Arcade events on the US disc. Every event uses a global scale of 100 %, so no AI car on a stock event ever runs below 70 % speed.

Can it freeze a car? In principle yes: a 100 % slow-down scales a car's time to zero. No stock event comes close, though. Only edited event data would reach it.
