GT2 License Tests

How Gran Turismo 2 runs a license test: what ends it with a pass, what fails it, how the time is measured, how a medal is decided and what the save keeps. Measured on driven, crashed and demonstration runs of the tests.

Three kinds of test

Each test record carries a kind byte. It decides what counts as the end of the test; everything else (the car, the course, the start, the targets) is plain data.

Stop in the box

kind 2 · A-1, A-2, B-1, B-2, B-3

Pass on the first tick (one 1/30 s physics step) the car is below 1 km/h with all four wheels inside the box (1,000 to 1,026 m along the course; A-2: 980 to 1,020 m) and on the road. Any wheel past the box end fails at once.

Reach the finish line

kind 3 · 42 point-to-point tests

Pass when the car crosses the last sector line of the license course. The time is the line time, worked out to a fraction of the tick.

Drive the laps

kind 5 · all S tests, IA-1, B-4, B-5

Pass at the lap line when the completed laps equal the record's lap count (1 or 2). Rolling starts count the run-up to the line.

One attempt

The check runs every tick after the clock. Penalties from the same tick (a crash, a course cut) fail the test before the goal is looked at. Nothing else ends a test: there is no wrong-way fail and no time limit while driving.

"car+0x77D" is a byte offset inside the car's record in memory: the test's result code lives there. Dirt courses (seven tests) have no course-cut fail at all; only a crash fails them. A run that passes but is slower than the slowest target is shown as FAIL, with its time, and still counts as a near miss or a top-five time.

The time

Lines

t = (clock_before + frac) / 3      ms
frac = the part of the tick before
       the line, by distance × 100

The clock counts 1/3000 s and starts at GO. In a license test it is never reset at a lap line, so the time runs from GO.

Stops

t = (clock_after + v × 100 / 1138) / 3
v = largest |velocity| + others / 4
    (1,138 = 1 km/h, v < 1,138)

The tick's end plus up to one more tick for the speed that is left: the same stopping tick scores better the slower the car still rolls.

Medals

target = m × 60000 + s × 1000 + hh × 10
medal  = time < target   (strict)

Targets have 10 ms steps, times 1 ms. 38.649 beats a 38.650 target; 38.650 does not.

“Always 0.023 s off”

A long-standing community report says license times are always 0.023 s off. Every link of the timing chain was checked: no fixed offset exists. Every line pass and every stop pass matches the formulas to the millisecond. The 23 ms players can see comes from a demonstration: the stop-test demos show the time stored in their file, but their runs replay to slightly different times on this disc.

After the drive

The run's replay plays at once. Only then does the result screen grade it, once, and only if it came from a fresh drive (a replay or a demonstration grades nothing).

grade = 4 gold · 3 silver · 2 bronze · 1 kids · 0 slower
kids time and no result yet → near misses + 1
     near misses reach the limit (5 / 10 / 20 / 30) → result 1 (pass)
     otherwise grade 0
result = max(old result, grade)
all ten results ≠ 0  → license held (first time: "LICENSE ACQUIRED")
all ten results = 4  → the license's prize car (first time only)
time in the test's top five → enter your name

The "kids" time is the slowest target, below bronze. License L can be taken once license L + 1 is held (B, A, IC, IB, IA, S). Events ask for the license in their own rules or any higher one. Nothing about licenses is stored except the ten results per license.
