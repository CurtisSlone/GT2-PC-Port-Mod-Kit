GT2 Simulation (SCUS-94488) · the menu car view

GT2's Showroom Car

How Gran Turismo 2 draws the turning 3D car on its dealer, car info, garage and shop pages and on Arcade's car selection. It is the race car renderer run through a small camera of its own. Every number here was read from the game and checked against the running game's memory.

One car, one camera, every frame

The menu page item with code 10 is a rectangle on the screen. Each frame GT2 builds a camera for that rectangle, draws the car the same way it does in races, draws a gray disc under it, and clips both to the rectangle.

1 · TURNStep the yawEvery frame the menu adds 12 to the camera's yaw (the left-right angle; one full turn every 341 frames) and holds the pitch at 110 (9.7 degrees down). Arcade adds 16.
2 · CAMERAOrbit at 9.30 mCamera matrix = RotY(-yaw) then RotX(-pitch); position = that matrix times (0, 0, 9.30 m). The car stays still; the camera circles it.
3 · LENSFit the rectangleWindow from the rectangle: width about 256 W / h, top 179, bottom -50, H 800 (H is the lens's focal distance). The rectangle's height sets the zoom; its width only crops.
4 · CARRace renderer, LOD 0The race car drawing with the most detailed model (LOD 0, level of detail) forced, the plain paint palette, shine 64, unsteered wheels, a shadow. No light: the paint is drawn as stored.
5 · FLOORA disc that does not turn24 triangles, 4 m radius, gray at the center fading to black, drawn from a second camera without yaw, sorted 5 m behind its own depth.

The camera, seen from the side

The camera's height is 9.30 sin(9.67 deg) = 1.56 m. In Arcade the pitch is 94 (8.26 deg); the post-race RESULTS screen picks a random pitch from 80 to 207 and a random starting yaw.

fx = 800 q0 / 4096, about 3.125 h; fy = 800 q1 / 4096, about 3.49 h. The car's floor point lands 0.28 h below the rectangle's center because the window's top is 179 and its bottom -50.

What the car is, and what it is not

Drawn as in a race

renderer
the same car drawing and clipping the race uses
LOD
0, always (CarDrawInfo +0 = 1, a byte offset in the car's draw record); wheels as 16-sided shapes
sort
GT2's depth bins (12.5 cm each) in the menu's middle layer, 1,088 entries
shine
sphere-map pass (a fake reflection) at strength 64; with dirt: (4096 - dirt) / 64
shadow
on (CarDrawInfo +3 = 1)
wheels
the model's wheel centers with the menu x column; no steer, no spin

Different from a race

light
none: the paint as stored (races darken it by the course's light rows)
model
always the day model; video memory slot 8 (texture at 512, 0)
shine
64 at most, two thirds of the race's 96
camera
turns around a still car; the floor uses a second camera with no yaw
clip
the middle layer's draw area set to the item's rectangle each frame
aspect
fy / fx = 1.117 on a screen that needs 1.25: about 11% flatter than true

Changing car, changing paint

New car. A page asks for a car with a delay counter (1 frame, or 3 after the car wash). When it runs out the menu loads the model, texture, logo and wheel image from the disc in one go (the menu waits), resets the yaw to 0x1500 and the pitch to 160 for one frame, and the car appears at the next drawn frame. No fade.

New paint. Left or Right on the paint chips changes the paint index; the next frame uploads the car's colors again. The model is not reloaded and the camera keeps turning.

Arcade. The two files stream from the disc and are unpacked while they load, alongside the menu. The port unpacks each one at once when it is in. Arcade also builds a mirrored camera for a floor reflection every frame and then draws nothing with it.

Idle. In Arcade and the race screens, once a counter passes 64,400 (about 18 minutes), the camera backs away by 0.5 m every frame.

Measured in the running game

The formulas above predict every number GT2 writes into its car view each frame. On the wheel shop and on Arcade's car selection they matched on every frame measured. A dealer page or the Car Wash opened directly (without coming from a page that picks a car) shows no car at all.

In the port

In races the port's remaster look already draws the cars with its own renderer. In the menus the car is still GT2's original polygons. How the port's renderer can take over this showroom car, with the same framing and motion, is on the native menu car page.
