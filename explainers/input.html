GT2 Input

How Gran Turismo 2 turns a controller into a car's steering, throttle and brake: the pad read every VBlank (each 60 Hz screen refresh), the controller set-up saved on the memory card, the 5-byte record a replay stores, and the rules that shape it into the car's controls. Measured on a digital pad and on a DualShock.

5bytes per car per tick in a replay
16steps per analog pedal
0.1 sfor a button pedal to reach full
±29 %stick dead zone

Five layers, each built from the one before

Every layer only reads what the layer above it wrote. The record in the middle is the boundary: a replay stores it, and a ghost, a replay and the player all hand the car the same thing.

"car+0x734" means byte offset 0x734 inside the car's record in memory; "wheel+0x60" the same inside each wheel's record.

The record a replay stores

Five bytes per car per tick (one 1/30 s physics step), stored after your button layout has been applied, so a replay plays the same whatever buttons you chose. The car never gets more from a player than this.

ByteWhatBecomes (12-byte record)
0flags: analog steer, analog throttle, analog brake, steering curve+0 flags
1buttons: left, right, throttle, brake, handbrake, reverse, shift up, shift downsteer ±2, pedals 0 / 1, +8 shift ±1, +9 reverse, +10 handbrake
2steering byte, 128 = center+2 steer = (128 − byte) × 32, ±4096
3throttle step 0..15+6 throttle = 4096 × step / 15 (table)
4brake step 0..15+4 brake, same table

The replay does not store every tick in full. "Run-length coding" stores a value once plus how many ticks it stayed the same:

stream entry  = flag byte (changed values · run & 7 · long run) + changed values + run length
run length    = 0xxxxxxx | 10xxxxxx x | 110xxxxx x x | 1110xxxx x x x   (big-endian, run >> 3)
full          = capacity − 17 bytes; closed 300 fields after the finish (60 in a license test)
playback      = the step that brings "ticks left" to 0 returns an empty record: the last tick never plays

Who drives a car

Two fields in the car's record, two jobs: one says who supplies the record, the other who turns it into controls.

Source car+0x18Driver car+0x489Car
20player 1: pad 0 live, its stream in a replay
30player 2 (two-player battle): pad 1
10ghost (rally, time trial): its stream, through the same player steps
02AI: the record is not even built
21player car with no controls (an event rule byte; never seen in play)

A player car on its cool-down lap or in the pit lane is driven by the AI, but its pad is still read and recorded.

The rules, as GT2 computes them

Buttons and key repeat

Each VBlank the game collects held, pressed and released bits until it reads them. A held button repeats on its 30th field (0.5 s), then every 7 fields.

pressed  |= (old ^ new) & new
c[i]     = held ? c[i] + 1 : 0
c[i]==37 → c[i] = 30;  c[i]==30 → repeat

Stick dead zone

A stick byte from 91 to 164 reads as center (about ±29 % of the throw); outside it the travel is stretched back to the ends.

v < 91   → v × 128 / 91
v < 165  → 128
else     → 128 + (v − 165) × 128 / 91

The controller set-up

Eleven slots per row; a byte below 0x80 is a button, above it an axis: the whole axis, one half, or both halves folded.

row = card block + 11 × (digital 0 · stick 1 · neGcon 2 · jogcon 3)
0xA0 low half:  ch = 2 × clamp(127 − v)
0xC0 high half: ch = 2 × clamp(v − 128)

Throttle and brake

Buttons ramp the pedal over 0.1 s each way (1365 of 4096 per tick, from values rebuilt at race start); handbrake is instant. Analog pedals jump to 12.5 % plus 87.5 % of the step.

digital: x = clamp(x ± 1365, 0, 4096)
analog:  x = x ? 7x/8 + 512 : 0
reverse: request + full throttle

Steering

The d-pad drives a spring toward the speed-sensitive lock; a stick sets a target (square law, or a curve while d-pad Up is held) that the wheel follows at a fixed rate.

digital: rate += K1(target − x) − K2 rate − K3 x
analog:  target = lock × s|s| / 4096
         x moves 49 / 4096 turn a tick

Rumble

One place sets it: the big motor follows a level from the front wheels, the road and hits; the small motor buzzes just after a hit. Off by the card's vibration setting, after your finish, in replays and while paused.

pad+0x5C = level_big << 4
pad+0x5E = level_small << 4
pad+0x60 = 2  (keep-alive, −1 a field)

Menus and pause

Menus confirm with Cross or Circle, cancel with Triangle or Square, and move on a press or a repeat. Only player 1 pauses: Start, or unplugging pad 0.

confirm = pressed & (Cross | Circle)
cancel  = pressed & (Triangle | Square)
move    = pressed | repeat
