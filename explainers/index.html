Inside GT2

How Gran Turismo 2 really works, measured and read from the game itself: the physics, the AI, events, cars, menus, graphics and sound. Plus what the port's modern physics and remaster look change. Pick any page; each one stands on its own.

An address like 0x801F0070 is a place in the PlayStation's 2 MB of RAM. An offset like +0x0A is a byte position inside a record (a car, a save, a course entry). They are there for modders; you can skip them.

Everything here is about GT2 Simulation, NTSC-U v1.0 (SCUS-94488), unless a page says otherwise.
