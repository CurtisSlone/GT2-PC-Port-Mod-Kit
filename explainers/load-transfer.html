GT2 port · modern physics · load transfer

Load transfer at its real size

GT2 moves load between the wheels only through the body's roll and pitch, at a moment arm that is the ride height (0.10-0.21 m) rather than a center-of-gravity (CG) height (about 0.5 m). So GT2 moves only about a third of the load a real car moves in a corner. This page shows the physical transfer the port's modern physics adds, measures it together with a more physical tire load curve (in GT2's own curve, a tire's grip per newton can rise with load; in the physical one it falls a little), and checks each LSD type against the real one. These changes are part of modern physics by default; classic keeps GT2's way.

Three paths, one total

A real suspension moves lateral load through the links (at the roll centers, without roll), through the springs and bars (by the roll), and through the unsprung mass. Their sum is (m_s h_s + m_u r) a_y / t whatever the roll centers are; the roll centers decide how much of it the bars and springs can move between the axles, and how much the body rolls. GT2 has only the elastic path, at the arm H.

Per-car heights from what GT2 has

GT2's frontal height is the car's overall height (Mustang 1.35 m, Civic 1.36 m, the Honda Z 1.67 m). The sprung CG is put at 30 % of the body above the ride height, the roll centers at 0.55 / 0.85 of the front / rear ride height, so lowering a car drops both.

The launch bog

GT2's launch clutch passes at least the engine's torque, so the engine only loses revs while it slips and rides 500 rpm above the wheel side. On the physical wheel the Vector W8's engine is pulled from 2,500 to 1,276 rpm for 3.1 s (0-100 8.50 s against 6.50 classic). Modern now holds the clutch rpm at launch as a driver would: 7.03 s.

What modern uses now

  • Yaw Control by GT2's own law on type 6 / 7 axles (the Lancer Evolutions, the Yaw Control part).
  • The physical tire load curve and the physical transfer, together, never the transfer alone. You feel the car lean on its outer tires: bars, springs and ride height become real balance tools, the limit drops about 9 %, a 2-way LSD calms the lift-off, open diffs spin the inside wheel out of tight corners.
  • The real ramp LSD's lock (preload plus ramp).
  • A driver's launch: fixes most of the launch bog.

Recordings remember which version of modern's settings they were made with and keep replaying with those.
