Gran Turismo 2 · Simulation disc US v1.0 · career and saves

One 31,900-byte block is the whole career

Money, days, licenses, every car with its tune, event results, options and controller set-up all live in one block of the PlayStation's RAM, starting at address 0x801C9340. The memory card gets a copy only when you choose Save Game. This page maps that block, every rule that changes it, and how the port can import it.

On the memory card

A PS1 card has 15 blocks of 8 KB. GT2's career file takes 4 of them; saved replays are a separate file. The file is a 512-byte header (icon and Japanese-encoded title), the save block, a standard CRC-32 checksum (a number computed from the data that shows whether it was damaged or edited), and leftover bytes.

Inside the save block

Drawn to scale. The garage is half the file: 100 slots of 164 bytes, each a car code, color, the full 0x84-byte (132-byte) set-up, the parts bought, the money put in, and its dirt. There is no mileage, oil or damage field anywhere. Offsets below (+0x...) are byte positions inside the block.

The career loop

GT Mode alternates between the menus and the race. The career changes only at the marked points; everything the menus show (licenses held, unlocks, completion, win ratio) is worked out from the stored fields each time.

The rules, in plain numbers

Money

  • Prizes add to credits, clamped to 0 … 99,999,999.
  • A new car costs its catalog price, a used car its lot price; selling returns a quarter of the catalog price, whatever you paid or fitted.
  • Wheels cost 2,000 Cr; the car wash 50 Cr (the sign says 5,000).
  • Championship bonuses reach your credits but never the "total prize money" figure.

Calendar and results

  • One day per event or license test entered, counted at the GO!, even if you leave before racing.
  • Win ratio = wins ÷ events entered, so backing out lowers it; average place uses finished races only.
  • Each event keeps its best place; completion = events won × 100 ÷ 223.
  • A new used-car lot every 10 days: 60 lots in a 600-day cycle, nothing random.

Licenses

  • Gold, silver, bronze by strictly beating the target time.
  • A "kids" time (the lowest pass) is given only after 5 to 30 tries (each test has its own limit); the best grade stays.
  • A license is held when all ten tests have a grade; no flag is stored.
  • All gold the first time adds the license's prize car.

Garage

  • 100 cars at most; a prize car won with a full garage is lost without a warning.
  • Prize cars are picked from the frame counter: the exact frame the result page appears.
  • Dirt carries between races as (acc << 12) / 600000; the rounding cleans a car by one unit on a clean drive.
  • Selling the current car leaves no current car.

Each of these rules was checked against the real game: applying the rule to a saved state gives exactly the state the game produces, on all 31,900 bytes.

Importing a GT2 card into the port

The port can keep its own save file. A GT2 card can be imported into it: the importer checks the CRC, reads only the career file, and maps each field to a value, reporting anything it had to clamp or drop.

Quirks in the original game

  • Dirt rounding: a clean drive cleans the car by one unit.
  • Win ratio divides by events entered, not races run.
  • A day passes at the GO! even when you back out.
  • Championship bonuses are missing from total prize money.
  • A hidden garage-move bug that could point the current car at the wrong car.
  • Prize cars are lost at 100 cars. The machine test record count can run past its limit of 8, and completion tops out at 98.20 %.
