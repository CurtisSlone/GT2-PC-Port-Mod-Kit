blank.mcd   an empty, formatted PlayStation memory card (128 KB). The installer copies it to card1.mcr if you
            have no card yet. Your saves go to card1.mcr; your own card from DuckStation or ePSXe works too
            (copy it over card1.mcr). UGT2R keeps a few files beside the card (ghosts, replay poses, the mods
            list): keep them with it. Card backups are off by default in this release: copy your card now and
            then, or start UGT2R with --card-backups cards-backup (guides/13-FLAGS-AND-SETTINGS.md).
